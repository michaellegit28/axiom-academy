Questions

Is this really 100% free?+

Yes. Free hosting, free content, no account required to read. No hidden paywall later.

Do I need calculus before starting?+

No — Volume 0 and Volume 1 build the mathematical foundation from the ground up before physics begins.

How long does the full series take?+

Roughly 4–5 years at 2 hours a day — comparable to an actual physics degree, because that's genuinely the scope.

Can I jump to a later volume?+

Each volume lists its prerequisites. You can skip ahead, but concepts build deliberately on earlier ones.
