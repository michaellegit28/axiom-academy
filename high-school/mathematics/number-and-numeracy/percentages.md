## Learning objectives
You should be able to convert among fractions, decimals and percentages; find percentages of quantities; calculate percentage change; and solve reverse-percentage problems.

## Meaning of percentage
Percent means “per hundred”:
$$25\%=\frac{25}{100}=0.25=\frac14.$$
To convert a percentage to a decimal divide by 100. To convert a decimal to a percentage multiply by 100.

## Percentage of an amount
Multiply by the percentage as a decimal:
$$35\%\text{ of }240=0.35(240)=84.$$

## Percentage increase and decrease
An increase of $r\%$ multiplies the original by $1+r/100$. A decrease multiplies it by $1-r/100$.
A ₦8,000 item discounted by 15% costs $8000(0.85)=₦6,800$.

## Percentage change
$$\text{percentage change}=\frac{\text{new}-\text{original}}{\text{original}}\times100\%.$$
A change from 80 to 100 is $20/80\times100\%=25\%$.

## Reverse percentages
If 15% is removed and the sale price is ₦6,800, the original is not found by simply adding 15%. Instead:
$$6800=0.85P\Rightarrow P=8000.$$

## Profit and loss
Profit = selling price − cost price. Profit percentage is normally calculated on cost price:
$$\frac{\text{profit}}{\text{cost price}}\times100\%.$$

## Repeated percentage changes
A 20% increase followed by a 20% decrease gives a multiplier $1.2\times0.8=0.96$, so the final value is 4% below the original.

## Common mistakes
- Confusing percentage points with percentage change.
- Using the new value instead of the original as the denominator for percentage change.
- Reversing a discount by adding the same percentage.
- Forgetting to convert percent to decimal before multiplication.

## Worked examples
**1.** Find 18% of ₦25,000: $0.18(25000)=₦4,500$.

**2.** Increase 500 by 12%: $500(1.12)=560$.

**3.** A value falls from 240 to 180. Percentage decrease is $60/240\times100\%=25\%$.

## Practice
1. Convert 0.375 to a percentage.
2. Find 35% of 240.
3. Increase 500 by 12%.
4. Decrease ₦15,000 by 8%.
5. A value changes from 80 to 100. Find the percentage increase.
6. A ₦12,000 item is discounted by 25%. Find the sale price.
7. A price after a 20% discount is ₦9,600. Find the original price.
8. A trader buys for ₦5,000 and sells for ₦5,750. Find the profit percentage.

## Key review
Percentages are fractions out of 100. For change, identify the original value carefully. For reverse percentages, divide by the relevant multiplier rather than reversing the percentage by addition.
