<h2>What a percentage means</h2><p>Percent means “per hundred”. Thus 25% means 25/100, which is 1/4.</p>
<h2>Finding a percentage of an amount</h2><p>Convert the percentage to a decimal or fraction, then multiply by the amount.</p>
<h2>Percentage increase and decrease</h2><p>For an increase, add the percentage change to 100% of the original. For a decrease, subtract the percentage change from 100%.</p>
<h2>Worked example</h2><p>A shirt costs ₦8,000 and is discounted by 15%. The discount is 0.15 × 8,000 = <strong>₦1,200</strong>. The sale price is ₦8,000 − ₦1,200 = <strong>₦6,800</strong>.</p>
<h2>Common mistake</h2><p>A 20% increase followed by a 20% decrease does not return to the original value. The second 20% is calculated from the increased amount.</p>
