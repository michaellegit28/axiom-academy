# Standard Form / Scientific Notation

Standard form writes a number as $a\times10^n$, where $1\le a<10$ and $n$ is an integer.

Examples: $4,500=4.5\times10^3$ and $0.00072=7.2\times10^{-4}$.

## Operations
Multiply coefficients and add exponents when multiplying powers of ten. For division, divide coefficients and subtract exponents.

Example: $(3\times10^5)(2\times10^3)=6\times10^8$.

## Practice
1. Write 82,000 in standard form.
2. Write 0.0064 in standard form.
3. Calculate $(4\times10^6)/(2\times10^2)$.

## Answers
1. $8.2\times10^4$.
2. $6.4\times10^{-3}$.
3. $2\times10^4$.
