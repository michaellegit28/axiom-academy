## Learning objectives
Convert ordinary numbers to and from standard form, compare their sizes, and multiply or divide numbers written in scientific notation.

## Definition
Standard form writes a number as
$$a\times10^n,$$
where $1\le a<10$ and $n$ is an integer. Scientific notation is the same representation.

## Converting numbers
Move the decimal point so exactly one non-zero digit remains before it. The number of places moved determines the exponent.
$$4500=4.5\times10^3,$$
$$0.00072=7.2\times10^{-4}.$$

A positive exponent indicates a number larger than 1; a negative exponent indicates a positive number smaller than 1.

## Multiplication
Multiply coefficients and add exponents:
$$(3\times10^5)(2\times10^3)=6\times10^8.$$
If the coefficient is outside $1\le a<10$, rewrite it. For example, $8\times10^4\times3=24\times10^4=2.4\times10^5$.

## Division
Divide coefficients and subtract exponents:
$$\frac{4\times10^6}{2\times10^2}=2\times10^4.$$

## Addition and subtraction
First express terms with the same power of ten, then add coefficients. For example,
$$3.2\times10^5+4.5\times10^4=3.2\times10^5+0.45\times10^5=3.65\times10^5.$$

## Common mistakes
- Writing a coefficient such as 45 in standard form.
- Reversing the sign of the exponent for small numbers.
- Adding exponents when adding numbers rather than multiplying powers.

## Practice
1. Write 82,000 in standard form.
2. Write 0.0064 in standard form.
3. Write $5.7\times10^6$ as an ordinary number.
4. Calculate $(4\times10^6)/(2\times10^2)$.
5. Calculate $(3\times10^4)(2\times10^{-2})$.
6. Calculate $3.2\times10^5+4.5\times10^4$.
7. Which is larger: $6.1\times10^7$ or $9.2\times10^6$?
8. Why is $0.45\times10^8$ not standard form?
