## Learning objectives
Find HCF and LCM using more than one method and choose the appropriate one for grouping and repeating-event problems.

## HCF
The highest common factor is the greatest positive factor shared by two or more numbers. For 18 and 24, common factors include $1,2,3,6$, so HCF = 6.

## LCM
The lowest common multiple is the smallest positive multiple shared by the numbers. Multiples of 12 and 18 first coincide at 36, so LCM = 36.

## Prime-factor method
For
$$18=2\times3^2,\qquad24=2^3\times3,$$
HCF uses the lowest powers: $2\times3=6$.
LCM uses the highest powers: $2^3\times3^2=72$.

## Listing method
Listing factors is practical for small HCF questions. Listing multiples can work for small LCM questions. Prime factors are more systematic for larger numbers.

## Applications
Use HCF when making the largest identical groups with no remainder. Use LCM when finding when repeating schedules coincide.

Two bells ring every 8 and 12 minutes. They ring together every LCM$(8,12)=24$ minutes.

## Common mistakes
- Confusing HCF with LCM.
- Using highest powers for HCF instead of lowest powers.
- Forgetting that LCM is the smallest positive common multiple.
