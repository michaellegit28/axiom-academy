# HCF and LCM

The highest common factor (HCF) is the greatest factor shared by two or more numbers. The lowest common multiple (LCM) is the smallest positive multiple shared by them.

## Prime-factor method
For $18=2\times3^2$ and $24=2^3\times3$:
- HCF uses the lowest powers: $2\times3=6$.
- LCM uses the highest powers: $2^3\times3^2=72$.

## Applications
Use HCF when grouping items into the largest identical groups. Use LCM when finding the next time repeating events coincide.

## Practice
1. Find HCF(36, 48).
2. Find LCM(12, 18).
3. Bells ring every 8 and 12 minutes. If they ring together now, when next together?

## Answers
1. 12.
2. 36.
3. In 24 minutes.
