# Recurring Decimals

A recurring decimal has a digit or block of digits that repeats indefinitely, such as $0.\overline3$ or $0.\overline{27}$.

## Fraction conversion
Let $x=0.\overline3$. Then $10x=3.\overline3$. Subtracting gives $9x=3$, so $x=1/3$.

For $0.\overline{27}$, let $x=0.\overline{27}$. Then $100x=27.\overline{27}$, so $99x=27$ and $x=3/11$.

## Practice
1. Convert $0.\overline6$ to a fraction.
2. Convert $0.\overline{45}$ to a fraction.
3. Explain why terminating decimals are rational numbers.

## Answers
1. $2/3$.
2. $5/11$.
3. They can be written as fractions with denominators that are powers of 10.
