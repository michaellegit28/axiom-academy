# Powers and Exponents

An exponent records repeated multiplication. $a^n$ means $a$ multiplied by itself $n$ times.

## Laws of indices
For suitable non-zero bases:
- $a^m a^n=a^{m+n}$
- $a^m/a^n=a^{m-n}$
- $(a^m)^n=a^{mn}$
- $a^0=1$
- $a^{-n}=1/a^n$

Example: $2^3\times2^4=2^7=128$.

Fractional indices connect powers and roots: $a^{1/2}=\sqrt a$.

## Practice
1. Simplify $x^4x^3$.
2. Simplify $5^7/5^3$.
3. Evaluate $3^{-2}$.

## Answers
1. $x^7$.
2. $5^4=625$.
3. $1/9$.
