## Learning objectives
Interpret ratios, simplify them, find missing terms, divide quantities in a given ratio, and solve scale problems.

## Meaning of a ratio
A ratio compares quantities. $3:5$ means 3 parts of the first quantity for every 5 parts of the second. Ratios compare like quantities, so convert units first when necessary.

## Simplifying ratios
Divide every term by the same common factor. For example,
$$18:24=3:4.$$
A ratio is in simplest form when no common factor greater than 1 remains.

## Equivalent ratios
Multiplying every term by the same non-zero factor preserves the ratio:
$$3:4=6:8=15:20.$$

## Sharing in a ratio
To divide ₦84 in the ratio $3:4$, there are $3+4=7$ parts. One part is ₦12, so the shares are ₦36 and ₦48.

## Ratios with three quantities
For $2:3:5$, there are 10 total parts. If the total is 250, one part is 25 and the quantities are 50, 75 and 125.

## Scale and maps
A scale of $1:50,000$ means 1 unit on the map represents 50,000 of the same unit in reality. Thus 4 cm represents 200,000 cm = 2 km.

## Common mistakes
- Adding ratio terms when simplifying instead of dividing all terms.
- Comparing quantities in different units.
- Forgetting that the order of a ratio matters.

## Practice
1. Simplify $28:42$.
2. Simplify $45:60:75$.
3. Divide 96 in the ratio $5:3$.
4. Divide 250 in the ratio $2:3:5$.
5. A map scale is $1:50,000$. What does 4 cm represent?
6. Boys:girls = $3:5$ and there are 32 students. Find each number.
7. If $x:12=5:3$, find $x$.
8. Convert $2.5$ m : $75$ cm to simplest form.

<details><summary>Show answers</summary>
1. $2:3$.
2. $3:4:5$.
3. 60 and 36.
4. 50, 75 and 125.
5. 2 km.
6. 12 boys and 20 girls.
7. $20$.
8. $10:3$.
</details>

## Key review
A ratio is a comparison, not a total. Simplify by a common factor, share by finding total parts, and convert units before comparing.
