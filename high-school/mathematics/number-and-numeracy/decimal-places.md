# Decimal Places

A decimal place is a position after the decimal point. Rounding to a specified number of decimal places keeps that many digits after the point.

Example: $7.3864$ rounded to 2 decimal places is $7.39$ because the third decimal digit is 6.

## Rounding method
1. Identify the last digit to keep.
2. Look at the next digit.
3. Round up if it is 5 or more; otherwise leave the kept digit unchanged.

## Practice
1. Round 18.764 to 1 decimal place.
2. Round 0.3486 to 2 decimal places.
3. Round 25.0049 to 3 decimal places.

## Answers
1. 18.8.
2. 0.35.
3. 25.005.
