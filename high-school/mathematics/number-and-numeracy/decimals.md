<h2>Place value</h2><p>Digits after the decimal point represent tenths, hundredths, thousandths and so on. Place value determines the size of a decimal.</p>
<h2>Comparing decimals</h2><p>Write zeros at the end when helpful. For example, 0.7 = 0.70, so 0.70 is greater than 0.65.</p>
<h2>Converting fractions and decimals</h2><p>A fraction with a denominator of 10, 100 or 1000 can be converted directly. Other fractions can often be converted by division.</p>
<h2>Worked example</h2><p>Convert 3/8 to a decimal. Divide 3 by 8 to get <strong>0.375</strong>.</p>
