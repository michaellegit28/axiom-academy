# Surds

A surd is an exact irrational root that cannot be simplified to a rational number. Common examples include $\sqrt2$ and $\sqrt3$.

## Simplifying surds
Factor out perfect squares: $\sqrt{48}=\sqrt{16\times3}=4\sqrt3$.

## Multiplication
$\sqrt a\sqrt b=\sqrt{ab}$ for non-negative $a,b$. Thus $\sqrt2\sqrt8=\sqrt{16}=4$.

## Rationalizing a denominator
For $1/\sqrt2$, multiply top and bottom by $\sqrt2$ to obtain $\sqrt2/2$.

## Practice
1. Simplify $\sqrt{75}$.
2. Simplify $2\sqrt7+3\sqrt7$.
3. Rationalize $1/\sqrt5$.

## Answers
1. $5\sqrt3$.
2. $5\sqrt7$.
3. $\sqrt5/5$.
