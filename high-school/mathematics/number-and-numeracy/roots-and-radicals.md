# Roots and Radicals

A square root of $a$ is a number whose square is $a$. Similarly, a cube root reverses cubing.

Examples: $\sqrt{49}=7$, $\sqrt[3]{27}=3$.

## Simplifying roots
Using prime factors, $\sqrt{72}=\sqrt{36\times2}=6\sqrt2$.

Radicals can be added only when they are like terms: $2\sqrt3+5\sqrt3=7\sqrt3$.

## Practice
1. Find $\sqrt{144}$.
2. Simplify $\sqrt{50}$.
3. Simplify $3\sqrt5+2\sqrt5$.

## Answers
1. 12.
2. $5\sqrt2$.
3. $5\sqrt5$.
