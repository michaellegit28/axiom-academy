# Rates and Unit Rates

## Learning objectives
- Calculate rates and unit rates.
- Convert between common rate units.
- Apply rates to speed, cost, density and productivity.

A rate compares quantities with different units. A unit rate gives the amount for one unit.

Example: A car travels 180 km in 3 h. Its average speed is $180/3=60$ km/h.

A shop sells 6 kg of rice for $₦9,000$. The unit price is $₦1,500$ per kg.

### Converting rates
To convert 72 km/h to m/s, use $72\times1000/3600=20$ m/s.

### Common mistakes
- Mixing units.
- Confusing a rate with a total quantity.
- Reporting a rate without units.

## Practice
1. Find the unit price of 8 items costing ₦2,400.
2. A cyclist covers 45 km in 1.5 h. Find the average speed.
3. Convert 90 km/h to m/s.

## Answers
1. ₦300 per item.
2. 30 km/h.
3. 25 m/s.
