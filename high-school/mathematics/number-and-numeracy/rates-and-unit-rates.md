## Learning objectives
Calculate rates, reduce them to unit rates, convert units correctly, and use rates to solve practical problems.

## Rate and unit rate
A rate compares quantities with different units. A unit rate gives the amount corresponding to one unit.
$$\text{rate}=\frac{\text{quantity}}{\text{time or other quantity}}.$$
A car travelling 180 km in 3 h has average speed $180/3=60$ km/h.

## Unit price
If 6 kg of rice costs ₦9,000, the unit price is ₦1,500/kg. Unit prices allow fair comparison between package sizes.

## Speed, distance and time
For constant average speed:
$$v=\frac dt,\qquad d=vt,\qquad t=\frac dv.$$
Keep units consistent.

## Converting rates
Since $1$ km = $1000$ m and $1$ h = $3600$ s,
$$72\text{ km/h}=72\times\frac{1000}{3600}=20\text{ m/s}.$$

## Other applications
Rates appear in density, flow, productivity, fuel consumption, wages and data transfer. Always state the units because they carry meaning.

## Common mistakes
- Dividing the wrong quantity by the other.
- Mixing hours and minutes or kilometres and metres.
- Reporting a numerical rate without units.
- Treating an average rate as if it described every instant.

## Worked examples
**1.** A cyclist covers 45 km in 1.5 h: $45/1.5=30$ km/h.

**2.** Eight identical items cost ₦2,400: unit price = ₦300/item.

**3.** Convert 90 km/h: $90/3.6=25$ m/s.

## Practice
1. Find the unit price of 8 items costing ₦2,400.
2. A cyclist covers 45 km in 1.5 h. Find average speed.
3. Convert 90 km/h to m/s.
4. A tap supplies 24 L in 6 minutes. Find the flow rate in L/min.
5. A worker earns ₦18,000 for 6 hours. Find the hourly rate.
6. A car travels at 80 km/h for 2.5 h. Find distance.
7. A machine produces 420 parts in 7 hours. Find productivity per hour.
8. Convert 15 m/s to km/h.
