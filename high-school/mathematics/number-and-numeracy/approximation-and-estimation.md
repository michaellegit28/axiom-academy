# Approximation and Estimation

Approximation replaces a value with a nearby, simpler value. Estimation uses sensible approximations to judge or calculate an answer quickly.

Examples: $398\approx400$ and $19.8\approx20$.

## Estimating calculations
For $49.7\times20.2$, estimate $50\times20=1000$.

An estimate is useful for checking whether an exact calculation is reasonable.

## Practice
1. Estimate $198\times51$.
2. Estimate $602/29$.
3. Explain why estimation is useful when checking a calculator answer.

## Answers
1. About 10,000.
2. About 20.
3. It provides a quick reasonableness check and can reveal major errors.
