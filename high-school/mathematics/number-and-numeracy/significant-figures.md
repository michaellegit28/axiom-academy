# Significant Figures

Significant figures record the meaningful digits in a measured or calculated value. Start at the first non-zero digit and count from there.

Examples: 0.00482 has 3 significant figures; 4,820 has 3 if written as an exact stated measurement to that precision.

## Rounding
To round to 3 significant figures, keep the first three significant digits and inspect the next digit. If it is 5 or more, increase the last kept digit.

Example: $38,742\to38,700$ to 3 significant figures.

## Practice
1. Round 0.007846 to 3 significant figures.
2. Round 56,391 to 2 significant figures.
3. How many significant figures are in 0.02050?

## Answers
1. 0.00785.
2. 56,000.
3. 4.
