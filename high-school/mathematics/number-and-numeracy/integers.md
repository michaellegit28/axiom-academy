## Learning objectives
By the end of this lesson you should be able to define integers, order them, use absolute value, and perform the four basic operations with signed numbers.

## The integer set
Integers are the positive whole numbers, zero, and their negatives:
$$\mathbb Z=\{\ldots,-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3,\ldots\}.$$ 
They describe quantities that can move in opposite directions, such as temperature, elevation, profit and loss.

## The number line
Moving right increases a value; moving left decreases it. Thus $-2>-5$ because $-2$ lies farther right. The opposite of $a$ is $-a$; opposite numbers are the same distance from zero.

## Absolute value
$|x|$ is the distance from zero. Hence $|7|=|-7|=7$. The distance between $a$ and $b$ is $|a-b|$.

## Adding and subtracting
Adding a positive moves right; adding a negative moves left. Subtraction means adding the opposite:
$$a-b=a+(-b).$$
For example, $-8-(-3)=-8+3=-5$.

## Multiplication and division
Same signs give a positive result; different signs give a negative result. For example,
$$(-6)(-4)=24,\qquad 18\div(-3)=-6.$$

## Order of operations
When several operations occur, use brackets, exponents, multiplication/division, then addition/subtraction. For example,
$$-3+2(5-8)=-3+2(-3)=-9.$$

## Applications
A temperature of $-4^\circ$C rising by $9^\circ$C becomes $5^\circ$C. A bank balance of ₦2,000 with a ₦3,500 withdrawal is $2000-3500=-₦1500$.

## Common mistakes
- Thinking $-8>-3$ because $8>3$; the number line shows the opposite.
- Losing the sign when subtracting a negative.
- Applying sign rules to addition; addition depends on direction and magnitude.
- Forgetting that $0$ is neither positive nor negative.

## Worked examples
**1.** Evaluate $-12+7-(-5)$. Convert subtraction of $-5$ to addition: $-12+7+5=0$.

**2.** Evaluate $(-4)(3)(-2)$. First $(-4)(3)=-12$, then $(-12)(-2)=24$.

**3.** Find the distance between $-7$ and $5$: $|5-(-7)|=12$.

## Practice

6. Evaluate $-2+3(4-7)$.

8. A temperature changes from $-6^\circ$C to $4^\circ$C. Find the change.

## Key review
Integers are ordered on a number line. Absolute value measures distance, subtraction adds an opposite, and multiplication/division use the same-sign/different-sign rule.
