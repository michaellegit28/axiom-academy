<h2>The integer number line</h2><p>Integers include ..., −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ... . Moving right increases a value; moving left decreases it.</p>
<h2>Comparing integers</h2><p>On a number line, the number farther to the right is greater. Therefore −2 is greater than −5 even though 5 is greater than 2.</p>
<h2>Operations with integers</h2><ul><li>Adding a positive moves right.</li><li>Adding a negative moves left.</li><li>Subtracting a number is equivalent to adding its opposite.</li><li>Multiplying or dividing numbers with the same sign gives a positive result.</li><li>Multiplying or dividing numbers with different signs gives a negative result.</li></ul>
<h2>Worked example</h2><p>Evaluate −8 − (−3). Subtracting −3 is the same as adding 3, so −8 + 3 = <strong>−5</strong>.</p>
