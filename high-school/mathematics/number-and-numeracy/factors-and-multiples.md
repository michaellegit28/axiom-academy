# Factors and Multiples

A factor divides a number exactly. A multiple is produced by multiplying a number by an integer.

For 12, the positive factors are $1,2,3,4,6,12$. Its multiples include $12,24,36,48$.

## Prime factorization
Write a number as a product of primes. For example, $60=2^2\times3\times5$.

Prime factorization is useful for finding HCF and LCM.

## Worked example
The factors of 36 are $1,2,3,4,6,9,12,18,36$. The first five positive multiples of 9 are $9,18,27,36,45$.

## Practice
1. List the factors of 24.
2. Write 84 as a product of prime factors.
3. Is 7 a factor of 91?

## Answers
1. $1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24$.
2. $2^2\times3\times7$.
3. Yes, $91=7\times13$.
