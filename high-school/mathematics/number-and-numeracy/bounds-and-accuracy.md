# Bounds and Limits of Accuracy

A rounded or measured value represents a range of possible exact values. These limits are called bounds.

If a length is recorded as 7.4 cm to the nearest 0.1 cm, then $7.35\le L<7.45$ cm.

The lower bound is included; the upper bound is excluded under the usual rounding convention.

## Using bounds
For an area calculated from measured dimensions, use lower bounds to find the minimum possible value and upper bounds for the maximum possible value.

## Practice
1. Give the bounds for 12.6 kg rounded to the nearest 0.1 kg.
2. A value is 50 to the nearest whole number. Give its bounds.
3. Why are bounds useful in measurement?

## Answers
1. $12.55\le x<12.65$ kg.
2. $49.5\le x<50.5$.
3. They describe the uncertainty introduced by measurement and rounding.
