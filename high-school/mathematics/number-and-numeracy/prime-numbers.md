# Prime Numbers

A prime number is a whole number greater than 1 with exactly two positive factors: 1 and itself. Examples are 2, 3, 5, 7 and 11.

## Important facts
- 2 is the only even prime.
- 1 is neither prime nor composite.
- Every integer greater than 1 has a unique prime factorization.

## Testing primality
To test whether a number $n$ is prime, it is enough to test prime divisors up to $\sqrt n$.

Example: To test 29, check 2, 3 and 5. None divides 29, so 29 is prime.

## Practice
1. Is 37 prime?
2. Explain why 1 is not prime.
3. Find the prime factors of 90.

## Answers
1. Yes.
2. It has only one positive factor.
3. $2\times3^2\times5$.
