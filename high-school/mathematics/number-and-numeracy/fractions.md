## Learning objectives
You should be able to interpret fractions, find equivalent forms, compare and simplify them, and perform all four operations.

## Meaning of a fraction
In $a/b$, $a$ is the numerator and $b\ne0$ is the denominator. A fraction can represent a part of a whole, a quotient, a ratio, or an operator. A proper fraction has numerator smaller than denominator; an improper fraction has numerator at least as large as denominator.

## Equivalent fractions and simplification
Multiplying or dividing numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number preserves value:
$$\frac34=\frac68=\frac9{12}.$$
Simplify using the greatest common factor. Thus $18/24=3/4$.

## Mixed numbers and improper fractions
Convert $2\frac13$ to an improper fraction:
$$2\frac13=\frac{2(3)+1}{3}=\frac73.$$
To convert $7/3$, divide $7$ by $3$: $2\frac13$.

## Comparing fractions
Use a common denominator, decimals, or cross multiplication. For positive denominators,
$$\frac ab>\frac cd\iff ad>bc.$$

## Addition and subtraction
Find a common denominator. For example,
$$\frac23+\frac14=\frac8{12}+\frac3{12}=\frac{11}{12}.$$

## Multiplication and division
Multiply numerators and denominators:
$$\frac25\times\frac{15}{8}=\frac34.$$
To divide by a fraction, multiply by its reciprocal:
$$\frac47\div\frac23=\frac47\times\frac32=\frac67.$$

## Fractions of quantities
“Three fifths of 40” means $\frac35\times40=24$. Always identify the whole before taking a fraction of it.

## Negative fractions
A negative sign may be written before, above, or in the numerator:
$$-\frac35=\frac{-3}{5}=\frac3{-5}.$$

## Common mistakes
- Adding denominators instead of finding a common denominator.
- Forgetting to invert the divisor when dividing.
- Cancelling terms across addition, such as incorrectly cancelling in $(x+2)/x$.
- Leaving an answer unsimplified when exact simplest form is expected.

## Worked examples
**1.** $5/6-1/4=10/12-3/12=7/12$.

**2.** $3/5\times10/9$. Cancel before multiplying: $3/9=1/3$ and $10/5=2$, giving $2/3$.

**3.** Find $2/7$ of 350: $350\times2/7=100$.

## Key review
Fractions are exact numbers. Simplify with common factors, use common denominators for addition/subtraction, multiply directly, and multiply by the reciprocal when dividing.
