<h2>What a fraction represents</h2><p>A fraction a/b represents a parts of a whole divided into b equal parts. The denominator cannot be zero.</p>
<h2>Equivalent fractions</h2><p>Multiplying or dividing the numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number preserves the value. For example, 3/4 = 6/8.</p>
<h2>Adding and subtracting</h2><p>Fractions with the same denominator can be combined directly. For different denominators, first find a common denominator.</p>
<h2>Worked example</h2><p>Calculate 2/3 + 1/4. A common denominator is 12. Convert the fractions: 2/3 = 8/12 and 1/4 = 3/12. Therefore the sum is <strong>11/12</strong>.</p>
<h2>Multiplication and division</h2><p>To multiply fractions, multiply numerators and denominators. To divide by a fraction, multiply by its reciprocal.</p>
