<h2>What is algebra?</h2><p>Algebra is a way of describing quantities and relationships using numbers, letters, and mathematical operations. A letter used to represent an unknown or changing quantity is called a variable.</p>
<h2>Key ideas</h2><ul><li>A <strong>variable</strong> represents a quantity whose value may be unknown or may change.</li><li>A <strong>constant</strong> has a fixed value.</li><li>An <strong>expression</strong> combines numbers, variables, and operations but does not contain an equals sign.</li><li>An <strong>equation</strong> states that two expressions are equal.</li></ul>
<h2>Worked example</h2><p>If a notebook costs x naira and you buy 3 notebooks, the total cost is <strong>3x</strong>. If you also pay 200 naira for a pen, the total is <strong>3x + 200</strong>.</p>
<h2>Check your understanding</h2><ol><li>What does a variable represent?</li><li>Write an expression for five more than twice n.</li><li>Explain the difference between an expression and an equation.</li></ol>
