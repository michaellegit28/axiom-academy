<h2>What is algebra?</h2><p>Algebra is a way of describing quantities and relationships using numbers, letters, and mathematical operations. A letter used to represent an unknown or changing quantity is called a variable.</p>
<h2>Key ideas</h2><ul><li>A <strong>variable</strong> represents a quantity whose value may be unknown or may change.</li><li>A <strong>constant</strong> has a fixed value.</li><li>An <strong>expression</strong> combines numbers, variables, and operations but does not contain an equals sign.</li><li>An <strong>equation</strong> states that two expressions are equal.</li></ul>
<h2>Worked example</h2><p>If a notebook costs x naira and you buy 3 notebooks, the total cost is <strong>3x</strong>. If you also pay 200 naira for a pen, the total is <strong>3x + 200</strong>.</p><ol><li>Identify the unknown: the notebook price is represented by x.</li><li>Represent three notebooks: 3x.</li><li>Add the fixed pen cost: 3x + 200.</li></ol><p>This illustrates how algebra turns a real situation into a mathematical expression.</p>
<h2>Check your understanding</h2><ol><li>What does a variable represent?</li><li>Write an expression for five more than twice n.</li><li>Explain the difference between an expression and an equation.</li></ol>
<h2>Practice set</h2><ol><li>Write an expression for 7 more than 4p.</li><li>In <strong>9m + 6</strong>, identify the variable, coefficient, and constant.</li><li>A bus ticket costs x naira. Write an expression for 5 tickets and a fixed service charge of 100 naira.</li></ol><details><summary>Show answers</summary><ol><li><strong>4p + 7</strong>.</li><li>Variable: <strong>m</strong>; coefficient: <strong>9</strong>; constant: <strong>6</strong>.</li><li><strong>5x + 100</strong>.</li></ol></details>
<h2>Quick review</h2><ul><li>Variables represent unknown or changing quantities.</li><li>Constants have fixed values.</li><li>Expressions describe quantities; equations state equality.</li></ul>
