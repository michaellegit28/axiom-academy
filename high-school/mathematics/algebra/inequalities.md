<h2>Inequality symbols</h2><ul><li><strong>&lt;</strong> means less than.</li><li><strong>&gt;</strong> means greater than.</li><li><strong>≤</strong> means less than or equal to.</li><li><strong>≥</strong> means greater than or equal to.</li></ul>
<h2>Solving inequalities</h2><p>Solve inequalities much like equations, but remember: when multiplying or dividing both sides by a negative number, <strong>reverse the inequality sign</strong>.</p>
<h2>Worked example</h2><p>Solve <strong>-2x &gt; 8</strong>. Divide by -2 and reverse the sign: <strong>x &lt; -4</strong>.</p><ol><li>Divide both sides by -2.</li><li>Because the divisor is negative, reverse &gt; to &lt;.</li><li>State the solution as x &lt; -4.</li></ol>
<h2>Number-line meaning</h2><p>A strict inequality such as x &gt; 3 excludes 3; an inclusive inequality such as x ≥ 3 includes 3. Number lines make this distinction visible.</p>
<h2>Check your understanding</h2><ol><li>Solve x + 5 ≤ 12.</li><li>Solve -3x ≥ 9.</li><li>Explain why the inequality sign changes when dividing by a negative number.</li></ol>
<h2>Practice set</h2><ol><li>Solve <strong>x - 4 &gt; 6</strong>.</li><li>Solve <strong>2x + 1 ≤ 9</strong>.</li><li>Solve <strong>-4x &gt; 12</strong>.</li></ol><details><summary>Show answers</summary><ol><li><strong>x &gt; 10</strong>.</li><li><strong>x ≤ 4</strong>.</li><li><strong>x &lt; -3</strong>.</li></ol></details>
<h2>Common mistake</h2><p>The inequality sign reverses only when multiplying or dividing by a negative number. Adding or subtracting the same quantity from both sides does not reverse it.</p>
