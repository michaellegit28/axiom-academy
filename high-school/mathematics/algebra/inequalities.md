<section class="section-block page-content-start study-lesson"><div class="section-heading"><div><span class="eyebrow">High School · Mathematics · Algebra</span><h1>Inequalities</h1><p>Represent and solve relationships using inequality symbols and number lines.</p></div></div><article class="content-card"><h2>Inequality symbols</h2><ul><li><strong>&lt;</strong> means less than.</li><li><strong>&gt;</strong> means greater than.</li><li><strong>≤</strong> means less than or equal to.</li><li><strong>≥</strong> means greater than or equal to.</li></ul><h2>Solving inequalities</h2><p>Solve inequalities much like equations, but remember: when multiplying or dividing both sides by a negative number, <strong>reverse the inequality sign</strong>.</p><h2>Worked example</h2><p>Solve -2x &gt; 8. Divide by -2 and reverse the sign: <strong>x &lt; -4</strong>.</p><h2>Number-line meaning</h2><p>A strict inequality such as x &gt; 3 excludes 3; an inclusive inequality such as x ≥ 3 includes 3. Number lines make this distinction visible.</p><h2>Check your understanding</h2><ol><li>Solve x + 5 ≤ 12.</li><li>Solve -3x ≥ 9.</li><li>Explain why the inequality sign changes when dividing by a negative number.</li></ol></article></section>
