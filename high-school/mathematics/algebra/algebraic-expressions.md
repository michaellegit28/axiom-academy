<section class="section-block page-content-start study-lesson"><div class="section-heading"><div><span class="eyebrow">High School · Mathematics · Algebra</span><h1>Algebraic Expressions</h1><p>Learn how terms, coefficients and operations combine to form expressions.</p></div></div><article class="content-card"><h2>Terms</h2><p>A term is a number, a variable, or a product of numbers and variables separated by addition or subtraction. In <strong>3x + 5y - 8</strong>, the terms are 3x, 5y and -8.</p><h2>Like and unlike terms</h2><p>Like terms have the same variables raised to the same powers. For example, 3x and -5x are like terms. x and x² are not like terms.</p><h2>Writing expressions</h2><ul><li>“seven more than x” → <strong>x + 7</strong></li><li>“three times y” → <strong>3y</strong></li><li>“twice n minus 4” → <strong>2n - 4</strong></li></ul><h2>Worked example</h2><p>For 4a + 3 - 2a + 7, group like terms: (4a - 2a) + (3 + 7) = <strong>2a + 10</strong>.</p><h2>Check your understanding</h2><ol><li>List the terms in 7x - 2y + 9.</li><li>Are 4m and -9m like terms?</li><li>Write an expression for “five less than three times k”.</li></ol></article></section>
