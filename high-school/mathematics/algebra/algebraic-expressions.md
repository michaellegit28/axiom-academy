<h2>Terms</h2><p>A term is a number, a variable, or a product of numbers and variables separated by addition or subtraction. In <strong>3x + 5y - 8</strong>, the terms are 3x, 5y and -8.</p>
<h2>Like and unlike terms</h2><p>Like terms have the same variables raised to the same powers. For example, 3x and -5x are like terms. x and x² are not like terms.</p>
<h2>Writing expressions</h2><ul><li>“seven more than x” → <strong>x + 7</strong></li><li>“three times y” → <strong>3y</strong></li><li>“twice n minus 4” → <strong>2n - 4</strong></li></ul>
<h2>Worked example</h2><p>For 4a + 3 - 2a + 7, group like terms: (4a - 2a) + (3 + 7) = <strong>2a + 10</strong>.</p><p><strong>Step 1:</strong> group terms with the same variable. <strong>Step 2:</strong> combine their coefficients. <strong>Step 3:</strong> combine numerical constants.</p>
<h2>Check your understanding</h2><ol><li>List the terms in 7x - 2y + 9.</li><li>Are 4m and -9m like terms?</li><li>Write an expression for “five less than three times k”.</li></ol>
<h2>Practice set</h2><ol><li>Simplify <strong>6x + 2 + 4x - 5</strong>.</li><li>Identify the like terms in <strong>3a + 7b - 2a + 4</strong>.</li><li>Translate “eight more than twice p” into an algebraic expression.</li></ol><details><summary>Show answers</summary><ol><li><strong>10x - 3</strong>.</li><li><strong>3a and -2a</strong> are like terms; 7b and 4 are each unlike those terms.</li><li><strong>2p + 8</strong>.</li></ol></details>
<h2>Common mistake</h2><p>Do not combine unlike terms. For example, <strong>3x + 4</strong> cannot be simplified to 7x because x and the constant 4 are different types of terms.</p>
