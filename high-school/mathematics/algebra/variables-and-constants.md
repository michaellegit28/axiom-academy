<section class="section-block page-content-start study-lesson"><div class="section-heading"><div><span class="eyebrow">High School · Mathematics · Algebra</span><h1>Variables & Constants</h1><p>Understand the symbols and fixed values used in algebra.</p></div></div><article class="content-card"><h2>Variables</h2><p>A variable is a symbol, usually a letter, used for a value that is unknown or can change. For example, x can represent the number of books in a bag.</p><h2>Constants</h2><p>A constant is a fixed number. In <strong>4x + 7</strong>, 4 and 7 are constants, while x is the variable.</p><h2>Coefficients</h2><p>A coefficient is the numerical factor multiplying a variable. In <strong>6y</strong>, 6 is the coefficient of y.</p><h2>Worked examples</h2><ul><li>In 9a + 3, a is the variable and 9 is its coefficient.</li><li>In 12 - m, 12 is a constant and m is a variable.</li></ul><h2>Check your understanding</h2><ol><li>Identify the variable and constant in 5p + 11.</li><li>What is the coefficient of t in 8t?</li><li>Give an algebraic expression containing one variable and two constants.</li></ol></article></section>
