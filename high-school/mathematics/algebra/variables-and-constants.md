<div class="section-heading"><div><span class="eyebrow">High School · Mathematics · Algebra</span><h1>Variables & Constants</h1><p>Understand the symbols and fixed values used in algebra.</p></div></div>
<article class="content-card">
<h2>Variables</h2><p>A variable is a symbol, usually a letter, used for a value that is unknown or can change. For example, <strong>x</strong> can represent the number of books in a bag.</p>
<h2>Constants</h2><p>A constant is a fixed number. In <strong>4x + 7</strong>, 4 and 7 are constants, while x is the variable.</p>
<h2>Coefficients</h2><p>A coefficient is the numerical factor multiplying a variable. In <strong>6y</strong>, 6 is the coefficient of y.</p>
<h2>Worked examples</h2><ul><li>In <strong>9a + 3</strong>, a is the variable and 9 is its coefficient.</li><li>In <strong>12 - m</strong>, 12 is a constant and m is a variable.</li></ul>
<h2>Quick review</h2><ul><li><strong>Variable:</strong> a symbol whose value may change or be unknown.</li><li><strong>Constant:</strong> a fixed value.</li><li><strong>Coefficient:</strong> the numerical factor multiplying a variable.</li></ul>
<h2>Check your understanding</h2><ol><li>Identify the variable and constant in <strong>5p + 11</strong>.</li><li>What is the coefficient of t in <strong>8t</strong>?</li><li>Give an algebraic expression containing one variable and two constants.</li></ol>
