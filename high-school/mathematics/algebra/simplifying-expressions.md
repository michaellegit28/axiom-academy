<section class="section-block page-content-start study-lesson"><div class="section-heading"><div><span class="eyebrow">High School · Mathematics · Algebra</span><h1>Simplifying Expressions</h1><p>Combine like terms and use the distributive property to write equivalent expressions.</p></div></div><article class="content-card"><h2>Combining like terms</h2><p>Add or subtract the coefficients of terms that have the same variable part. For example, 7x + 2x = <strong>9x</strong>.</p><h2>Distributive property</h2><p>Multiply a factor outside parentheses by every term inside: <strong>3(x + 4) = 3x + 12</strong>.</p><h2>Worked example</h2><p>Simplify 2(3x - 5) + 4x. First distribute: 6x - 10 + 4x. Then combine like terms: <strong>10x - 10</strong>.</p><h2>Order of operations</h2><p>When evaluating an expression, follow the agreed order of operations. Parentheses are handled before exponents, multiplication/division, and addition/subtraction.</p><h2>Check your understanding</h2><ol><li>Simplify 5x + 3x - 2.</li><li>Expand 4(y - 3).</li><li>Simplify 2(a + 5) + 3a.</li></ol></article></section>
