<h2>Combining like terms</h2><p>Add or subtract the coefficients of terms that have the same variable part. For example, 7x + 2x = <strong>9x</strong>.</p>
<h2>Distributive property</h2><p>Multiply a factor outside parentheses by every term inside: <strong>3(x + 4) = 3x + 12</strong>.</p>
<h2>Worked example</h2><p>Simplify 2(3x - 5) + 4x. First distribute: 6x - 10 + 4x. Then combine like terms: <strong>10x - 10</strong>.</p><ol><li>Distribute 2 across both terms: 6x - 10.</li><li>Bring down the +4x.</li><li>Combine 6x and 4x.</li></ol>
<h2>Order of operations</h2><p>When evaluating an expression, follow the agreed order of operations. Parentheses are handled before exponents, multiplication/division, and addition/subtraction.</p>
<h2>Check your understanding</h2><ol><li>Simplify 5x + 3x - 2.</li><li>Expand 4(y - 3).</li><li>Simplify 2(a + 5) + 3a.</li></ol>
<h2>Practice set</h2><ol><li>Simplify <strong>9x - 4x + 7</strong>.</li><li>Expand <strong>5(2y - 3)</strong>.</li><li>Simplify <strong>3(2a + 4) - a</strong>.</li></ol><details><summary>Show answers</summary><ol><li><strong>5x + 7</strong>.</li><li><strong>10y - 15</strong>.</li><li><strong>5a + 12</strong>.</li></ol></details>
<h2>Common mistake</h2><p>When distributing a negative factor, apply it to every term inside the brackets. For example, <strong>-2(x + 3) = -2x - 6</strong>.</p>
