# Geometry

Geometry studies shapes, sizes, positions, angles, and relationships in space.

## Angles & Lines
Study complementary and supplementary angles, vertically opposite angles, and angle relationships formed by parallel lines.

**Worked example:** If two angles on a straight line are `x` and `65°`, then `x + 65° = 180°`, so `x = 115°`.

## Triangles
Learn triangle classification, angle sum, congruence, similarity, and basic properties of right triangles.

**Key fact:** The interior angles of a triangle sum to `180°`.

## Quadrilaterals & Polygons
Study properties of squares, rectangles, parallelograms, rhombi, trapezia/trapezoids, and regular polygons.

**Key fact:** The sum of interior angles of an n-sided polygon is `(n - 2) × 180°`.

## Circles
Study radius, diameter, circumference, area, chords, arcs, and basic angle relationships in circles.

## Area & Perimeter
Learn how to calculate perimeter and area for common 2D shapes and how to decompose compound figures.

## Coordinate Geometry
Study points, gradients, distance, midpoint, and equations of straight lines on the coordinate plane.

### Quick review
Always identify the shape or relationship first, select the appropriate theorem or formula, and check that the units and answer are reasonable.
