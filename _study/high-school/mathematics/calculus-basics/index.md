# Calculus Basics

Calculus introduces ways to describe change and accumulation. This foundation focuses on introductory limits and differentiation.

## Introduction to Limits
A limit describes the value a function approaches as the input approaches a particular value. Limits help define continuity and derivatives.

## Basic Differentiation
The derivative describes instantaneous rate of change. For powers of x, the power rule gives `d/dx(x^n) = nx^(n-1)` for positive integer n in this introductory setting.

**Worked example:** `d/dx(x³) = 3x²`.

## Applications of Differentiation
Derivatives can be used to find gradients, rates of change, and stationary points. Interpret the derivative in the context of the problem rather than treating it as a symbol-only calculation.

### Quick review
Understand what a limit represents, apply basic derivative rules carefully, and connect the derivative to rate of change or gradient.
