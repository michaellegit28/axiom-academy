# Variables & Constants

## Study
A variable represents a value that can change or an unknown value. A constant keeps the same value. In `4x + 7`, `x` is the variable, `4` is its coefficient, and `7` is the constant.

### Substitution
To evaluate an expression, replace each variable with its given value and follow the order of operations.

### Worked example
If `x = 3`, then `4x + 7 = 4(3) + 7 = 19`.

## Practice
1. Identify the coefficient and constant in `9p - 5`.
2. Evaluate `3m + 2` when `m = 6`.

## Key takeaways
Distinguish the role of a variable, coefficient, and constant. Substitute carefully and use parentheses when replacing a variable with a negative value.
