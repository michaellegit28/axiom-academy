# Introduction to Algebra

## Study
Algebra uses symbols and variables to represent quantities and relationships. A variable stands for a value that may be unknown or may change. A constant has a fixed value.

### Core ideas
- A **term** is a number, variable, or product of numbers and variables.
- A **coefficient** is the numerical factor multiplying a variable.
- An **expression** combines terms with operations such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division.
- An **equation** states that two expressions are equal.

### Worked example
In `5x + 3`, `x` is the variable, `5` is the coefficient, `3` is the constant, and the whole expression has two terms.

## Practice
1. Identify the variable, coefficient, and constant in `7y + 4`.
2. How many terms are in `3a - 2b + 9`?

## Key takeaways
Algebra gives us a language for describing unknown quantities, patterns, and relationships. Learn to identify the parts of an expression before manipulating it.
