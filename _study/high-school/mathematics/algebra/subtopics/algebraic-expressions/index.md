# Algebraic Expressions

## Learn
An algebraic expression combines numbers, variables and operations. Expressions do not contain an equality statement.

Translate language carefully: “five more than x” is `x + 5`, while “five times x” is `5x`.

## Worked example
Evaluate `2a + 3b` when `a = 4` and `b = 2`:

`2(4) + 3(2) = 8 + 6 = 14`.

## Common mistake
Order matters in subtraction. “Three less than x” means `x - 3`, not `3 - x`.

## Quick review
- Identify the terms in `4x + 3y - 8`.
- Translate “twice n plus 6” into an expression.

## Check your understanding
Evaluate `3m - 4` when `m = 9`.
