# Introduction to Algebra

## Learn
Algebra uses numbers, symbols and variables to describe quantities, patterns and relationships. A variable represents a value that can change or an unknown value.

### Key concepts
- **Variable:** a symbol representing an unknown or changing quantity.
- **Constant:** a fixed value.
- **Term:** a number, variable, or product of numbers and variables.
- **Coefficient:** the numerical factor of a term.
- **Expression:** a combination of terms and operations without an equality statement.

## Worked example
Simplify `3x + 5x - 2`.

The terms `3x` and `5x` are like terms, so combine their coefficients:

`3x + 5x - 2 = 8x - 2`.

## Common mistake
Do not combine unlike terms such as `3x` and `5` because they do not have the same variable part.
