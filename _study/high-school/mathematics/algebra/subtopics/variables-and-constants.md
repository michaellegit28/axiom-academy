# Variables & Constants

## Learn
A variable represents an unknown or changing value. A constant has a fixed value. In `4x + 7`, `x` is the variable, `4` is its coefficient, and `7` is the constant.

## Worked example
If `x = 3`, then `4x + 7 = 4(3) + 7 = 19`.

## Quick review
Distinguish the variable, coefficient, and constant before evaluating an expression.
