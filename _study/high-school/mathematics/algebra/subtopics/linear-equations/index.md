# Linear Equations

## Learn
An equation states that two expressions are equal. Solve a linear equation by performing equivalent operations on both sides while keeping the equality balanced.

## Worked example
Solve `2x + 5 = 17`:

`2x = 12`

`x = 6`

Check: `2(6) + 5 = 17`, so the solution is correct.

## Common mistake
Do the same operation to both sides. Do not move a term across the equality sign without accounting for the operation.

## Quick review
Solve `x + 9 = 15`.

## Check your understanding
Solve `3x - 4 = 20` and substitute your answer back into the original equation.
