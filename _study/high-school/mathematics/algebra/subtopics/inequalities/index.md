# Inequalities

## Learn
Inequalities compare quantities using `<`, `>`, `≤` or `≥`. Their solution can represent a range of values rather than one value.

Solve inequalities much like equations, but reverse the inequality sign when multiplying or dividing by a negative number.

## Worked example
Solve `-2x > 8`:

Divide by `-2` and reverse the sign:

`x < -4`.

## Common mistake
Forgetting to reverse the sign when multiplying or dividing by a negative number changes the solution incorrectly.

## Quick review
Solve `x + 3 ≤ 10`.

## Check your understanding
Solve `-3x ≥ 12` and state the direction of the inequality after dividing.
