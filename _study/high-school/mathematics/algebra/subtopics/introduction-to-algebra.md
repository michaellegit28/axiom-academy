# Introduction to Algebra

## Learning goals
- Recognise variables, constants, terms and coefficients.
- Read and write simple algebraic expressions.
- Identify like and unlike terms.

## Core study
Algebra uses symbols and numbers to represent quantities and relationships. A variable represents a value that may change or is unknown. A constant has a fixed value. A term is a number, variable, or product of numbers and variables.

## Worked example
In `4x + 7`, `x` is the variable, `4` is the coefficient, and `7` is the constant.

## Common mistake
Do not treat `4x` as `4 + x`. The coefficient 4 multiplies x.

## Quick review
- Variable: a symbol representing a value.
- Constant: a fixed value.
- Coefficient: a numerical factor of a term.
- Like terms: terms with the same variable part.

## Practice
1. Identify the coefficient and constant in `6y + 9`.
2. Which pair are like terms: `3x` and `8x`, or `3x` and `8y`?
