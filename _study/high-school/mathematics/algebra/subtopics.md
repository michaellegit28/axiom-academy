# Algebra — Subtopics

Choose a subtopic to study.

## Introduction to Algebra
Algebra uses numbers, symbols and variables to represent quantities, patterns and relationships.

## Variables & Constants
Learn how variables, constants and coefficients work together in algebraic expressions.

## Algebraic Expressions
Translate words into algebraic notation, evaluate expressions and identify terms.

## Simplifying Expressions
Combine like terms and use the distributive property to rewrite equivalent expressions.

## Linear Equations
Solve equations by maintaining equality on both sides and check solutions by substitution.

## Inequalities
Solve and represent inequalities using `<`, `>`, `≤` and `≥`, reversing the sign when multiplying or dividing by a negative number.
