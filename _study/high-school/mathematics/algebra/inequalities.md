# Inequalities

## Study
Inequalities compare quantities using `<`, `>`, `≤`, or `≥`. Their solution is usually a set of values rather than one value.

### Important rule
Solve inequalities much like equations, but reverse the inequality sign whenever you multiply or divide by a negative number.

### Worked example
`-2x > 8`. Dividing by `-2` reverses the sign, giving `x < -4`.

## Practice
1. Solve `x + 3 ≤ 10`.
2. Solve `-4x ≥ 12`.

## Key takeaways
Know the meaning of each inequality symbol and remember the sign reversal rule for multiplication or division by a negative number.
