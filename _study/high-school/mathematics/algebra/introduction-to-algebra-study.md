# Introduction to Algebra

## Learn
Algebra uses numbers, symbols and variables to describe quantities, patterns and relationships. A variable represents a value that may change or is unknown. A constant has a fixed value.

## Key concepts
- **Term:** a number, variable, or product of numbers and variables.
- **Coefficient:** the numerical factor multiplying a variable.
- **Expression:** a combination of terms connected by operations.
- **Equation:** a statement that two expressions are equal.

## Worked example
Simplify `3x + 5x - 2`. The like terms combine to give `8x - 2`.

## Common mistake
Do not combine unlike terms such as `3x` and `5`.

## Quick review
Identify variables, constants, coefficients and terms before simplifying or solving.
