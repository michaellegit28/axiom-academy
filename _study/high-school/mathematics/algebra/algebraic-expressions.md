# Algebraic Expressions

## Study
An algebraic expression is a mathematical phrase made from numbers, variables, and operations. Unlike an equation, an expression does not state that two quantities are equal.

### Translating words
- five more than `x` → `x + 5`
- three times `x` → `3x`
- twice `y` decreased by 4 → `2y - 4`

### Worked example
Evaluate `2a + 3b` when `a = 4` and `b = 2`: `2(4) + 3(2) = 8 + 6 = 14`.

## Practice
1. Write an expression for “seven less than twice n.”
2. Evaluate `5x - 2` when `x = 9`.

## Key takeaways
Translate language precisely, identify the operations, and substitute values only after the expression has been written correctly.
