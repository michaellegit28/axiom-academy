# Linear Equations

## Study
An equation states that two expressions have equal value. Solving means finding the value of the variable that makes the equation true.

### Balance principle
Whatever operation is performed on one side must also be performed on the other side.

### Worked example
`2x + 5 = 17` → subtract 5: `2x = 12` → divide by 2: `x = 6`.

### Check
Substitute the solution into the original equation: `2(6) + 5 = 17`, so the solution is correct.

## Practice
1. Solve `3x - 4 = 14`.
2. Solve `5(x + 2) = 25`.

## Key takeaways
Keep equations balanced, simplify carefully, and always check a solution in the original equation when practical.
