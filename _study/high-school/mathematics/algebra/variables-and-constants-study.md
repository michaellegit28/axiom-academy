# Variables and Constants

## Learn
A variable represents a value that can change or an unknown value. A constant has a fixed value. A coefficient is the numerical factor attached to a variable.

## Worked example
In `4x + 7`, `x` is the variable, `4` is the coefficient and `7` is the constant. If `x = 3`, then `4(3) + 7 = 19`.

## Common mistake
The coefficient and variable are separate parts of a term: in `4x`, the coefficient is `4`, not `4x`.

## Quick review
Be able to identify variables, constants and coefficients and substitute a given value for a variable.
