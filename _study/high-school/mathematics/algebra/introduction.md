# Introduction to Algebra

## Learn
Algebra represents quantities, patterns and relationships using numbers, symbols and variables. A variable can represent an unknown or changing quantity, while a constant has a fixed value.

## Key concepts
- A term is a number, variable, or product of numbers and variables.
- A coefficient is the numerical factor multiplying a variable.
- Like terms have the same variable parts and can be combined.
- An expression does not contain an equality statement.

## Worked example
Simplify `3x + 5x - 2`.

Combine the like terms: `3x + 5x = 8x`.

**Answer:** `8x - 2`.

## Common mistake
Do not combine unlike terms. For example, `3x + 2y` cannot be simplified to `5xy`.
