# Algebra

Algebra is the study of quantities, patterns and relationships represented with numbers, symbols and variables.

## 1. Introduction to Algebra
### Study
Algebra lets us describe unknown quantities and general rules. A **variable** represents a quantity that can change; a **constant** has a fixed value. An algebraic expression combines numbers, variables and operations.

### Key ideas
- A term is a number, variable, or product of numbers and variables.
- A coefficient is the numerical factor of a term.
- Like terms have the same variable parts and can be combined.

### Worked example
For `3x + 5x - 2`, combine like terms: `8x - 2`.

## 2. Variables & Constants
### Study
A variable such as `x` can represent an unknown or changing value. A constant such as `7` keeps the same value. In `4x + 7`, `4` is the coefficient of `x` and `7` is the constant.

### Worked example
If `x = 3`, then `4x + 7 = 4(3) + 7 = 19`.

## 3. Algebraic Expressions
### Study
Expressions do not contain an equality statement. Translate words into symbols carefully: “five more than x” is `x + 5`, while “five times x” is `5x`.

### Worked example
Evaluate `2a + 3b` when `a = 4` and `b = 2`: `8 + 6 = 14`.

## 4. Simplifying Expressions
### Study
Simplification means rewriting an expression in an equivalent, simpler form. Combine like terms and use the distributive property when needed.

### Worked example
`3(x + 4) + 2x = 3x + 12 + 2x = 5x + 12`.

## 5. Linear Equations
### Study
An equation states that two expressions are equal. Solve by performing equivalent operations on both sides while keeping the equality true.

### Worked example
`2x + 5 = 17` → `2x = 12` → `x = 6`.

### Check
Substitute `x = 6`: `2(6) + 5 = 17`, so the solution is correct.

## 6. Inequalities
### Study
Inequalities compare quantities using `<`, `>`, `≤` or `≥`. Solve them like equations, but **reverse the inequality sign when multiplying or dividing by a negative number**.

### Worked example
`-2x > 8` → `x < -4`.

## Quick review
1. Identify variables, coefficients and constants.
2. Translate and evaluate expressions accurately.
3. Combine like terms when simplifying.
4. Keep both sides balanced when solving equations.
5. Reverse an inequality when multiplying or dividing by a negative number.
