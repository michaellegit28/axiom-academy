# Variables and Constants

## Learn
A variable represents a quantity that can change or an unknown value. A constant has a fixed value. In `4x + 7`, `x` is the variable, `4` is its coefficient, and `7` is the constant.

## Worked example
If `x = 3`, then `4x + 7 = 19`.

## Common mistake
The coefficient is not the whole term. In `6y`, the coefficient is `6`; the term is `6y`.

## Quick review
- Variable: a changing or unknown quantity.
- Constant: a fixed value.
- Coefficient: the numerical factor of a variable.
