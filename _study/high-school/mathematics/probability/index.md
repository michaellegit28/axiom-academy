# Probability

Probability measures how likely an event is to occur.

## Probability Basics
For equally likely outcomes, `P(E) = favourable outcomes / total outcomes`.

**Worked example:** A fair die has six outcomes. The probability of rolling an even number is `3/6 = 1/2`.

## Sample Spaces
A sample space lists all possible outcomes of an experiment. Keep outcomes distinct and complete before calculating probabilities.

## Events
An event is a set of outcomes from a sample space. Events can be simple, compound, mutually exclusive, or overlapping depending on the situation.

## Probability Rules
For complementary events, `P(not A) = 1 - P(A)`. For mutually exclusive events, `P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B)`.

### Quick review
Define the sample space, identify the event, select the correct probability rule, and express the result on the interval from 0 to 1.
