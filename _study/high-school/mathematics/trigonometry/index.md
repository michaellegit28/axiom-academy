# Trigonometry

Trigonometry studies relationships between angles and sides of triangles and their applications.

## Trigonometric Ratios
For a right triangle, relative to an acute angle θ: `sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse`, `cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse`, and `tan θ = opposite/adjacent`.

## Right Triangles
Use the Pythagorean theorem, `a² + b² = c²`, together with trigonometric ratios to find missing sides and angles.

**Worked example:** If the opposite side is 6 and hypotenuse is 10, `sin θ = 6/10 = 0.6`.

## Angles of Elevation & Depression
Translate a real-world diagram into a right triangle, identify the reference angle, and choose the ratio that connects the known and unknown quantities.

## Sine & Cosine Rules
Use the sine rule when an angle-side opposite pair is available. Use the cosine rule when the given information supports a side-angle-side or three-side calculation.

## Trigonometric Identities
Identities are equations true for all values where both sides are defined. A central identity is `sin²θ + cos²θ = 1`.

### Quick review
Draw the triangle or model first, label known quantities, select a suitable ratio or rule, and keep angle units consistent.
