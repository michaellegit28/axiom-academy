## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:
- Answer JAMB-style MCQs on units, dimensions, and measurement
- Solve kinematics problems (speed, velocity, acceleration) in under 90 seconds
- Apply Newton's laws to standard JAMB problem types
- Use dimensional analysis to verify formulas
- Recognize common JAMB traps and distractors

---

## 1. Quick Facts: Units & Dimensions

**Fundamental quantities:** Length (m), Mass (kg), Time (s), Current (A), Temperature (K)

**Common derived units:**
- Force: kg·m/s² = **N**
- Work/Energy: N·m = **J**
- Power: J/s = **W**
- Pressure: N/m² = **Pa**

**Dimensional analysis shortcut:** If a formula is correct, both sides must have the same dimensions.

**Example:** Check if $v^2 = 2as$ is dimensionally correct.
- LHS: $[v^2] = (L/T)^2 = L^2/T^2$
- RHS: $[a][s] = (L/T^2)(L) = L^2/T^2$ ✓

> **JAMB Trap:** They often give formulas with wrong dimensions. Always check.

## 2. Motion in One Dimension

### Key Equations (constant acceleration)

$$ v = u + at $$
$$ s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2 $$
$$ v^2 = u^2 + 2as $$
$$ s = \frac{(u + v)}{2}t $$

**Under gravity:** $a = g = 10$ m/s² (JAMB standard). Downward is positive if you define it so.

### Speed vs Velocity vs Acceleration

| Term | Meaning | Scalar/Vector |
|------|---------|---------------|
| Speed | Distance / time | Scalar |
| Velocity | Displacement / time | Vector |
| Acceleration | Change in velocity / time | Vector |

> **JAMB Trap:** "A car travels 100 m north then 100 m south. What is the displacement?" Answer: 0 m (not 200 m).

## 3. Newton's Laws

**First Law:** A body remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by a force.

**Second Law:** $F = ma$. Force causes acceleration proportional to force, inversely proportional to mass.

**Third Law:** Action and reaction are equal and opposite. They act on *different* bodies.

> **JAMB Trap:** "A book rests on a table. The weight of the book and the normal force from the table are action-reaction pairs." **False.** They act on the *same* body (the book). The reaction to the book's weight is the book's gravitational pull on the Earth.

## 4. Problem-Solving Strategy for JAMB

**The 90-Second Method:**

1. **Read the question twice.** Underline what is given and what is asked.
2. **Write the formula** before plugging numbers.
3. **Check units.** Convert everything to SI before calculating.
4. **Estimate first.** Eliminate absurd options before calculating.
5. **Calculate carefully.** Watch for squares, square roots, and factors of 2.
6. **Check the answer.** Does it make physical sense?

## 📝 JAMB Practice Questions

### Topic 1: Units & Measurement

**Q1.** Which of the following is a vector quantity?
- A. Speed
- B. Distance
- C. Mass
- D. Velocity

**Q2.** The dimension of power is:
- A. ML²T⁻²
- B. ML²T⁻³
- C. MLT⁻²
- D. ML⁻¹T⁻²

**Q3.** A measurement is recorded as 0.00450 m. How many significant figures does it contain?
- A. 2
- B. 3
- C. 4
- D. 5

### Topic 2: Motion

**Q4.** A car accelerates uniformly from rest at 2 m/s² for 5 seconds. The distance traveled is:
- A. 10 m
- B. 25 m
- C. 50 m
- D. 100 m

**Q5.** A ball is thrown vertically upward with a velocity of 20 m/s. How long does it take to return to the thrower's hand? ($g = 10$ m/s²)
- A. 2 s
- B. 4 s
- C. 5 s
- D. 10 s

**Q6.** The velocity-time graph of a body moving with uniform acceleration is a:
- A. Straight line through the origin
- B. Straight line with positive slope
- C. Parabola
- D. Circle

### Topic 3: Forces

**Q7.** A force of 10 N acts on a body of mass 2 kg. The acceleration produced is:
- A. 0.2 m/s²
- B. 5 m/s²
- C. 12 m/s²
- D. 20 m/s²

**Q8.** A body of mass 5 kg is moving with a velocity of 10 m/s. A constant force acts on it for 2 s, after which its velocity is 20 m/s. The magnitude of the force is:
- A. 12.5 N
- B. 25 N
- C. 50 N
- D. 100 N

**Q9.** Which of the following statements is correct according to Newton's third law?
- A. Action and reaction act on the same body
- B. Action and reaction are equal only when bodies are at rest
- C. Action and reaction are equal and opposite and act on different bodies
- D. Action is always greater than reaction

**Q10.** A block of mass 10 kg rests on a rough horizontal surface. The coefficient of static friction is 0.4. The minimum horizontal force required to move the block is: ($g = 10$ m/s²)
- A. 4 N
- B. 25 N
- C. 40 N
- D. 100 N

---

## ✅ Solutions & Explanations

**Q1. D** — Velocity has magnitude and direction.

**Q2. B** — Power = Work/time = (Force × distance)/time = (MLT⁻² × L)/T = ML²T⁻³.

**Q3. B** — Leading zeros don't count. 4, 5, 0 are significant = 3 sig figs.

**Q4. B** — $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2 = 0 + \frac{1}{2}(2)(5)^2 = 25$ m.

**Q5. B** — Time up = $v/g = 20/10 = 2$ s. Time down = 2 s. Total = 4 s.

**Q6. B** — Uniform acceleration means constant slope. If it starts from rest, it passes through origin; if not, it has an intercept. The safest general answer is "straight line with positive slope."

**Q7. B** — $a = F/m = 10/2 = 5$ m/s².

**Q8. B** — $a = (v - u)/t = (20 - 10)/2 = 5$ m/s². $F = ma = 5 \times 5 = 25$ N.

**Q9. C** — Newton's third law: equal, opposite, different bodies.

**Q10. C** — $F_{\text{min}} = \mu_s N = \mu_s mg = 0.4 \times 10 \times 10 = 40$ N.

## ⚡ Speed Tips for JAMB

| Situation | Shortcut |
|-----------|----------|
| Object dropped from height $h$ | Time to fall: $t = \sqrt{2h/g}$ |
| Object thrown up at $v$ | Max height: $h = v^2/(2g)$ |
| Converting km/h to m/s | Divide by 3.6 |
| Density conversion g/cm³ → kg/m³ | Multiply by 1000 |
| Work done by constant force | $W = Fd\cos\theta$ (if force and displacement at angle $\theta$) |

> **Remember:** JAMB gives $g = 10$ m/s² unless stated otherwise. Use 10, not 9.8.
