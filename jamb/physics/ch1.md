By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Apply Newton's laws to standard JAMB problem types

## 1. Quick Facts: Units & Dimensions

**Fundamental quantities:** Length (m), Mass (kg), Time (s), Current (A), Temperature (K)

**Common derived units:** Force: kg·m/s² = **N**; Work/Energy: N·m = **J**; Power: J/s = **W**; Pressure: N/m² = **Pa**.

**Dimensional analysis shortcut:** If a formula is correct, both sides must have the same dimensions.

## 2. Motion in One Dimension

$$ v = u + at $$
$$ s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2 $$
$$ v^2 = u^2 + 2as $$
$$ s = \frac{(u + v)}{2}t $$

**Under gravity:** $a = g = 10$ m/s² (JAMB standard).

## 3. Newton's Laws

**First Law:** A body remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by a force.

**Second Law:** $F = ma$.

**Third Law:** Action and reaction are equal and opposite and act on different bodies.

## 4. Problem-Solving Strategy for JAMB

1. Read the question carefully.
2. Identify the given quantities and unknown.
3. Write the appropriate formula.
4. Convert to SI units.
5. Calculate and check whether the result is physically sensible.

## 📝 JAMB Practice Questions

### Topic 1: Units & Measurement

**Q1.** Which of the following is a vector quantity?
- A. Speed
- B. Distance
- C. Mass
- D. Velocity

**Q2.** The dimension of power is:
- A. ML²T⁻²
- B. ML²T⁻³
- C. MLT⁻²
- D. ML⁻¹T⁻²

### Topic 2: Motion

**Q3.** A car accelerates uniformly from rest at 2 m/s² for 5 seconds. The distance traveled is:
- A. 10 m
- B. 25 m
- C. 50 m
- D. 100 m

### Topic 3: Forces

**Q4.** A force of 10 N acts on a body of mass 2 kg. The acceleration produced is:
- A. 0.2 m/s²
- B. 5 m/s²
- C. 12 m/s²
- D. 20 m/s²

## ✅ Solutions

**Q1. D** — Velocity has magnitude and direction.

**Q2. B** — Power has dimensions ML²T⁻³.

**Q3. B** — $s = \frac{1}{2}at^2 = 25$ m.

**Q4. B** — $a = F/m = 5$ m/s².
